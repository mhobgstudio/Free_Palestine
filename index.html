🕊️ About This Memorial

This site exists to honor and remember every civilian who has lost their life in Gaza — regardless of faith, background, or age. Each number is a human being with dreams, family, and a story cut short.

By showing their names, ages, and backgrounds, we aim to restore the humanity behind the statistics. Memory is resistance. Witness is obligation.
